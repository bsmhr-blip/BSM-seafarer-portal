Built around what a seafarer has to do next

The old portal listed 16 modules side by side. Helm asks one question first — what needs this seafarer's action today? — and shows those items as cards on Home. Each card opens the exact step to finish it.

Act now — Home

Vessel plans to accept, expired or missing pre-joining documents, readiness to confirm, required feedback, returned claims, safety alerts and one-time e-tickets.

Coming up — Home & Voyage

Next flight, joining date, medical appointment and planned courses on one timeline, with milestone reminders once you accept a plan — no countdown that breaks when dates move.

When you need it — Menu

Icon tiles for payslips, allotments, claims, downloads, jobs, Vessel Tracker and Wellbeing. My Documents and profile changes stay in Profile.

Where every portal module lives in Helm

Nothing from the current seafarer portal is lost. Each module has a home, and the urgent parts of it surface on the Action Center automatically.

Home

Icon tiles, To Do, milestones, matching sea and shore jobs, coming up.

News (tab)

Company announcements, fleet updates, circulars and safety alerts.

Actions (+)

BSM Training Center (request a slot via crewing), CMS, scan, medical, claims, readiness.

Voyage (tab)

Assignment and offer letter, milestones, pre-joining, travel, crew, history.

Profile › My Documents

Passports, CDC, licences, visas and certificates with expiry tracking; family, readiness, approvals.

Menu › Money

Payslips with remarks, allotments, beneficiaries (OTP), expense claims, currency, downloads.

Menu › Wellbeing

Anonymous check-ins, support, shore leave, port info, reminders; shore-team dashboard.

Menu › Vessel Tracker

All BSM fleet vessels on a map or list with voyage details.

Built for life at sea

Designed for satellite links, night watches and gloved hands.

Offline first

Uploads, claims, feedback and plan responses queue in an outbox and send in priority order when VSAT or Starlink returns.

Secure by default

Biometric unlock, OTP on allotment and beneficiary changes, one-time e-ticket downloads and a full download log.

Light or dark

Dark for night watches, light for sunlight on deck. Follows the phone setting or your choice.
